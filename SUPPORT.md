# Get help with PROTOCOL

Use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/KadenB20/PROTOCOL-Community/issues) for
bugs and suggestions. Community support is handled as time allows.

Tell us your PROTOCOL version, your Windows version, what you were trying to do,
what happened, and the steps that lead to the problem. A cropped screenshot can
help. Check existing reports before opening a new one.

Keep your training, health, and personal records private. Do not attach databases,
backups, private photos, bloodwork, or credentials. If an update fails, keep your
data folder and pre-update backup. Do not reset your workspace to troubleshoot.

Feature suggestions are welcome. Explain what you need to record, plan, or review
and where the current workflow gets in your way. Suggestions do not promise a
particular release date.
