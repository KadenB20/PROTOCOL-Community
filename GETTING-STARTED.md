# Getting started

Start with the parts you already track. You do not need to fill in every section.
The screenshots use made-up example data, not recommended targets.

## Record a day

Open Daily Tracker and choose the day. Add the bodyweight, sleep, activity,
nutrition and other details you want to keep. You can return to an earlier day
to finish or correct it. If you don't know a value, leave it blank rather than
entering zero.

![A populated week in Daily Tracker, including bodyweight, sleep and nutrition](images/daily-tracker.png)

## Add meals and training

Build a meal plan from saved foods, then choose the plan you used in Daily Tracker.
Adjust logged meals to reflect what you actually ate. Use Grocery lists to plan
your shopping and Pantry to keep track of the food you have. When adding purchases
or logging meals, check the amounts before updating your Pantry totals.

In Training, save your program and record the workout after training. Use a previous
workout or program as a starting point. Save a draft if you are not finished.

## Review your week

Use the Check-ins tab in Daily Tracker to create a form with the questions you
want to answer each week. Add your answers, look back at the week's results, and
note anything you want to change or follow up on. You can make monthly, quarterly,
and annual forms too.

## Look across a phase

Open Phase Timeline to compare weeks of bodyweight, nutrition, training and other
recorded results with your targets. Choose the dates and rows you want to review.
Phase names and notes help explain what changed during that period.

![Weekly bodyweight and nutrition across a fictional cutting phase in Phase Timeline](images/phase-timeline.png)

## Keep only what you need visible

In Settings, hide the sections you don't use and choose your units, time zone,
and appearance. Hiding a section won't delete anything you've already saved there.

## Keep a backup

Use Backup & Restore to create and check a backup, especially before an update.
Keep a separate copy somewhere private. Records, photos and backups are not
encrypted by PROTOCOL. [Read about privacy](PRIVACY.md).
