# PROTOCOL Community privacy

PROTOCOL Community stores your training, nutrition, health, photos, and other
records on your Windows device. No PROTOCOL account is required. The Community
application does not send these records to a PROTOCOL cloud service and does not
include usage analytics, advertising, or automatic crash uploads.

## Local records and backups

Records, Vault files, and backups are not encrypted by PROTOCOL. Anyone who can
read those files may be able to read their contents. Protect your Windows account,
device, and backup location. If you put an export or backup in a synced folder,
that folder's provider handles it under its own privacy terms.

The data folder is shown in Settings → Workspace & Data → Technical details.
Use Backup & Restore to make and check backups. Removing the application does not
itself erase the separate record folder.

## Optional network activity

Update notices are off until you turn them on. When enabled, PROTOCOL checks the
public GitHub release feed at most once a day while the app is open. You can also
choose Check for updates. Requests contain no training records, profile name, or
health data. GitHub receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP
address and the request time. Turning notices off stops future automatic checks.

Optional food and exercise providers receive the search terms, barcodes, or
identifiers needed for your selected lookup. Opening source links, support pages,
or downloads uses your browser and the destination's own privacy terms.

## Support and sharing

Nothing is uploaded automatically with a support request. GitHub issues and any
screenshots you attach are public. Never post a backup, full database, bloodwork
report, private photo, address, access token, or another person's information.
Crop screenshots to the problem and remove identifying details first.

Exports and sharing actions are under your control. Check their contents and the
recipient before sending them.
